# Careers in Cyber – TryHackMe

**Room Type:** Beginner  
**Focus:** Cybersecurity career paths, roles, and required skill sets

---

## Overview
This room provides an overview of common cybersecurity roles and how they fit into the security ecosystem. It helps learners understand different career paths, required skills, and how technical and non-technical roles contribute to organizational security.

## What I Learned
- The major cybersecurity career paths such as SOC analyst, penetration tester, and security engineer  
- Skill requirements for Blue Team, Red Team, and GRC roles  
- How foundational IT and networking knowledge supports cybersecurity careers  
- The importance of continuous learning and hands-on practice  

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **Role Mapping** – Understanding responsibilities across cybersecurity positions  
- **Skill Alignment** – Matching technical skills to specific career paths  
- **Learning Path Planning** – Identifying areas to focus on for career growth  

## Key Concepts Covered
- Blue Team vs Red Team vs GRC
- Entry-level vs advanced cybersecurity roles
- Technical and soft skills in security
- Career progression in cybersecurity
- Continuous learning

## Takeaway
This lab helps beginners navigate the cybersecurity field by clarifying career options and guiding skill development toward real-world roles.

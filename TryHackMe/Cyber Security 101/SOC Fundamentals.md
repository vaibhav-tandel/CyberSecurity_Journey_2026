# SOC Fundamentals – TryHackMe

**Room Type:** Beginner  
**Focus:** Security Operations Center (SOC) operations, alert monitoring, and incident response fundamentals

---

## Overview
This room provides an introduction to how a Security Operations Center (SOC) functions within an organization. It focuses on SOC responsibilities, alert handling workflows, and the role of analysts in detecting and responding to security incidents.

## What I Learned
- The primary purpose of a SOC and its role in organizational security
- Differences between SOC analyst levels (L1, L2, L3) and their responsibilities
- How security alerts are generated, triaged, and escalated
- The importance of logging, monitoring, and defined response procedures

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **SIEM** – Collects and correlates logs to identify suspicious activity
- **Alert Triage** – Evaluating alerts to determine severity and legitimacy
- **Incident Response Process** – Standardized approach to handle security events

## Key Concepts Covered
- Security Operations Center (SOC)
- Continuous monitoring
- Alerting and escalation
- Incident detection and response
- Blue Team fundamentals

## Takeaway
This lab establishes essential SOC knowledge and prepares beginners for real-world SOC analyst and Blue Team roles.

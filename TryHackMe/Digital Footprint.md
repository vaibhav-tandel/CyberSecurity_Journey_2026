# Digital Footprint – TryHackMe

**Room Type:** Beginner  
**Focus:** Open-source intelligence (OSINT), online exposure, and personal data awareness

---

## Overview
This room introduces the concept of a digital footprint and how online activity leaves traceable information. It focuses on how publicly available data can be collected and analyzed, and why managing online exposure is important for security and privacy.

## What I Learned
- The difference between active and passive digital footprints  
- How publicly shared information can be leveraged for reconnaissance  
- Why attackers use digital footprints during the early stages of an attack  
- How excessive online exposure increases the risk of social engineering and profiling  

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **OSINT Concepts** – Collecting intelligence from publicly available sources  
- **Profile Analysis** – Reviewing social media and online presence for exposed data  
- **Reconnaissance Techniques** – Understanding how attackers gather background information  
- **Exposure Awareness** – Identifying and minimizing unnecessary online data leakage  

## Key Concepts Covered
- Digital footprint (active vs passive)
- Open-source intelligence (OSINT)
- Reconnaissance and profiling
- Social engineering risk
- Privacy and exposure management

## Takeaway
This lab highlights how everyday online activity contributes to a digital footprint and why understanding OSINT is critical for reducing attack surface and improving personal and organizational security.

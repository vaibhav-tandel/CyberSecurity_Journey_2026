# Introduction to EDR – TryHackMe

**Room Type:** Beginner  
**Focus:** Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) concepts, endpoint visibility, and threat detection

---

## Overview
This room introduces Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) and its role in modern defensive security. It explains how EDR solutions monitor endpoint activity to detect, investigate, and respond to malicious behavior.

## What I Learned
- The purpose of EDR and how it differs from traditional antivirus solutions  
- How endpoint telemetry is collected and analyzed for threat detection  
- Common attack behaviors EDR tools are designed to identify  
- The importance of endpoints as a primary target in real-world attacks  

## Tools & Techniques Used
- **EDR Platform** – Monitors endpoint processes, files, and network activity  
- **Behavior-Based Detection** – Identifies threats based on suspicious actions rather than signatures  
- **Endpoint Telemetry Analysis** – Reviewing detailed system activity for investigation  

## Key Concepts Covered
- Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR)
- Endpoint telemetry
- Behavioral threat detection
- Incident investigation
- Blue Team endpoint security

## Takeaway
This lab builds a foundational understanding of EDR, a critical technology used by SOC teams to detect and respond to endpoint-based attacks.

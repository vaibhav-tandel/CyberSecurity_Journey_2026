# SOC Role in Blue Team – TryHackMe

**Room Type:** Intro

## Overview
This room explains the role of a Security Operations Center (SOC) within the Blue Team and how it helps protect an organization from cyber threats.

---

## What I Learned
- Purpose and structure of a SOC
- Responsibilities of SOC analysts
- How SOC teams handle security incidents


## SOC Responsibilities
- Monitor security alerts and logs  
- Detect and investigate suspicious activity  
- Respond to and escalate incidents  
- Maintain security visibility across systems  


## SOC Analyst Levels
- **Tier 1** – Monitor alerts and perform initial analysis  
- **Tier 2** – Deep investigation and incident response  
- **Tier 3** – Threat hunting and advanced analysis  

---

## Takeaway
The SOC is the **first line of defense** for detecting and responding to cyber attacks.
